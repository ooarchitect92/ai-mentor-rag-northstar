# CMA Demo Lesson: Variance Analysis

Variance analysis compares actual performance with budgeted or standard performance.

A variance is favorable when it improves profit compared with the standard. A variance is unfavorable when it reduces profit compared with the standard.

Key formula:
Sales Price Variance = (Actual Price - Standard Price) × Actual Quantity

Material Price Variance = (Actual Price - Standard Price) × Actual Quantity Purchased

Material Quantity Variance = (Actual Quantity Used - Standard Quantity Allowed) × Standard Price

Labor Rate Variance = (Actual Rate - Standard Rate) × Actual Hours

Labor Efficiency Variance = (Actual Hours - Standard Hours Allowed) × Standard Rate

Simple explanation:
Variance analysis tells managers where performance differed from plan. It helps separate price issues from usage or efficiency issues.

Example:
If actual material price is ₹105 per kg, standard price is ₹100 per kg, and actual quantity purchased is 1,000 kg, then material price variance is:
(105 - 100) × 1,000 = ₹5,000 unfavorable.

Practice questions:
1. Actual labor rate is ₹520 per hour, standard rate is ₹500 per hour, and actual hours are 300. Calculate labor rate variance.
2. Actual material used is 2,200 kg, standard quantity allowed is 2,000 kg, and standard price is ₹80 per kg. Calculate material quantity variance.
