ACCA questions

Plan from your real starting point.

Can I start ACCA after school?

Your starting point depends on current ACCA entry requirements and your academic background. The counselling team can help you understand the route relevant to you.

Could I receive exemptions?

Exemptions depend on prior qualifications and current ACCA rules. Confirm them formally before building your study plan.

Can I prepare online?

Yes. NorthStar supports online learning, and you can ask about available Bengaluru classroom options.
