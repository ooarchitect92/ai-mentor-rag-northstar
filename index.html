Frequently asked

Start with the questions that matter.

Which qualification should I choose: CMA, CPA or ACCA?

It depends on your education, career direction, geography and preferred work. CMA is strong for management accounting and business finance, CPA for accounting and audit pathways, and ACCA for a broad international accounting route.

Can I study while working or attending college?

Yes. NorthStar supports flexible learning paths, including online options, designed for students and working professionals.

Do you provide online counselling?

Yes. You can speak with the team remotely, or visit the Bengaluru centre.
